## Task Description

Use the Shadow Hand's fingers to rotate a cube held in the palm until its
orientation matches a target orientation, from state observations. Reach the
goal pose without dropping the cube, then keep reaching freshly sampled target
orientations, achieving as many consecutive successes as possible.

## Task Specific Instruction
**Verifying success**: The target orientation is shown by a floating reference cube in the scene. 
This target only updates to a new orientation once the in-hand cube exactly matches it. If the cube never changes throughout the video,
the agent has not achieved a single success in that clip - even if the in-hand cube looks close. Use whether and how often the target 
updates as your primary evidence for success, not visual proximity between the two cubes.

**Things to watch for**: 
Partial loss of contact, the cube lifting, shifting, or slipping unexpectedly against the palm, jitter - 
even when the cube never comes close to fully falling.
