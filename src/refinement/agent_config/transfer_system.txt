
Transfer learning: this is an iterative process in which the policy is carried over between iterations. From the second iteration onward, the policy trained with your new `compute_reward` does NOT start from random weights: its actor and critic are initialized from the best policy of the previous iteration, which was trained under a previous version of the reward. Training then continues under your new reward for the usual budget. Each reward you write is therefore both the objective for the policy trained now and the starting point that the next reward will build on, so design rewards that refine the existing behaviour rather than restarting from scratch.
